## Connect

```bash
curl "https://api.plugkit.co/v1/connect/bluesky?profileId=YOUR_PROFILE_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key"
```

Open the returned `authUrl` and sign in. For an account hosted outside
bsky.social, pass its handle: `&handle=alice.example.com`.

## Publish

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.plugkit.co/v1/posts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "profileId": "PROFILE_ID", "accountIds": ["ACCOUNT_ID"],
        "content": "Hello Bluesky 🦋", "publishNow": true }'
```

- **300 characters** max (counted as graphemes) — refused above, never truncated.
- Text only, **or** 1 to 4 images, **or** one video (100 MB max). Large images
  are recompressed to fit Bluesky's 1 MB limit.
- A link in the text gets a preview card automatically.
- A published post can be removed with `POST /posts/{id}/unpublish`.

## Replies and mentions

Replies and mentions show up in the [engagement feed](/guides/engagement), where
you can answer them. Bluesky has no hide or delete for other people's replies.

## Analytics

Likes, reposts and replies per post, plus followers. Bluesky exposes no views or
impressions.

## Not supported

No DMs in PlugKit yet: no inbox, broadcasts or sequences.
