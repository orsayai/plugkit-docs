Threads connects a **Threads profile** through Meta's Threads API. It is a
separate connection from Instagram: nothing carries over from a connected
Instagram account.

## Connect

```bash
curl "https://api.plugkit.co/v1/connect/threads?profileId=YOUR_PROFILE_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key"
```

Open the returned `authUrl`, approve on Threads' screen, and the profile is
connected after the callback.

## Publish

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.plugkit.co/v1/posts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "profileId": "PROFILE_ID", "accountIds": ["ACCOUNT_ID"],
        "content": "Shipping today 🧵", "replyControl": "everyone", "publishNow": true }'
```

- **500 characters** max — a longer `content` is refused, never truncated.
- Text only, **or** one image/video, **or** a carousel of 2 to 20 items.
- Options: `replyControl`, `topicTag`, `linkAttachment` (preview card on a text
  post), `poll`, `firstComment`. See
  [Publishing](/guides/publishing#threads) for the details.
- A published post can be removed with `POST /posts/{id}/unpublish`.
- Threads allows 250 published posts per 24 hours per profile.

## Replies and mentions

Replies under your posts and posts that mention you show up in the
[engagement feed](/guides/engagement). You can reply and hide; Threads doesn't
let you delete someone else's reply.

## Not supported

Threads has no DM API: no inbox, broadcasts, sequences or comment → DM
automations.
