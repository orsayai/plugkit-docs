LinkedIn is a **publishing** integration.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publish" icon="pen">Text, 1–20 images, one video, or a poll.</Card>
  <Card title="Visibility" icon="eye">Public, connections, or signed-in members.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect

```bash
# the member's own profile
curl "https://api.plugkit.co/v1/connect/linkedin?profileId=YOUR_PROFILE_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key"

# company Pages the member administers
curl "https://api.plugkit.co/v1/connect/linkedin?profileId=YOUR_PROFILE_ID&target=page" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key"
```

Open the returned `authUrl` and approve. With `target=page`, each Page picked
becomes its own account.

## Publish

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.plugkit.co/v1/posts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "profileId": "PROFILE_ID", "accountIds": ["ACCOUNT_ID"],
        "content": "We are hiring.", "linkedinVisibility": "PUBLIC", "publishNow": true }'
```

- Text only, **or** 1 to 20 images, **or** one video.
- `linkedinVisibility`: `PUBLIC`, `CONNECTIONS` or `LOGGED_IN`.
- `poll` (`question`, 2–4 `options`, duration rounded to 1, 3 or 7 days) on a
  post without media.

## Not supported

LinkedIn exposes no messaging API, and no analytics at this access level: no
inbox, no comments, no metrics.
