---
title: "Building on PlugKit for your SaaS"
description: "Manage your own customers' social accounts through one PlugKit account."
---

If your product publishes or messages on behalf of **your** customers, PlugKit
is the layer underneath: one PlugKit account, one API key on your server, and
one profile per customer.

## The model

```
Your PlugKit account ── API key (server-side only)
 ├── Profile "Customer A"  →  their Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn…
 ├── Profile "Customer B"  →  their Facebook Page, WhatsApp…
 └── …
```

1. When a customer signs up in your app, create a profile and store its `_id`
   next to your customer record:
   ```bash
   curl -X POST https://api.plugkit.co/v1/profiles \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{ "name": "Customer A (your internal id 4812)" }'
   ```
2. Every later call names that profile (`profileId`) or one of its accounts
   (`accountId`).

<Warning>
  **Isolation is your job.** API keys aren't scoped to a profile: every key sees
  every profile of your PlugKit account. Never expose a key to the browser, and
  always resolve `profileId` / `accountId` from your own customer record, never
  from user input.
</Warning>

## Connecting your customer's accounts

Two options:

**From your UI.** Call `GET /connect/{platform}?profileId=…` from your server and
send your customer to the returned `authUrl`. Once they approve, the platform
redirects to a PlugKit confirmation page. PlugKit doesn't redirect back to your
app, so open the `authUrl` in a popup or a new tab and close it when you detect
the new account (see below).

**With a connect link.** `POST /connect-invites` with the `profileId` returns a
`url` valid 30 days. Send it to your customer: they pick their platforms on a
PlugKit-hosted page, and the accounts land in their profile. Good for agencies
onboarding clients by email.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.plugkit.co/v1/connect-invites \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "profileId": "PROFILE_ID" }'
```

## After the connection

There is no `account.connected` webhook. Poll the profile's accounts while your
customer is in the flow (every few seconds), then stop:

```bash
curl "https://api.plugkit.co/v1/accounts?profileId=PROFILE_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_your_key"
```

One authorization can create several accounts (a Facebook login granting three
Pages gives three accounts).

Tokens can die on the platform side (password change, revoked app). Those
accounts switch to `needsReconnection: true` **without** a webhook — check
accounts periodically and before publishing, and send the customer back through
`GET /connect/{platform}?profileId=…&accountId=…` to reconnect the same account.

## Routing webhooks

Register one [webhook](/guides/webhooks) endpoint for your whole account.
Message, comment and account events carry `accountId` but not `profileId`:
keep an `accountId → profileId → your customer` map, refreshed from
`GET /accounts`. Post events carry the full post, `profileId` included.

## Limits to plan for

- **Rate limits are shared** by all your customers: 20 publications / messages /
  comment replies per minute, 60 writes and 300 requests per minute in total.
  Queue publications on your side and honour `retry-after`. See
  [API conventions](/concepts/api-conventions#rate-limits).
- **Platform quotas** apply per connected account (e.g. 100 Instagram
  publications per 24 hours).
- **Plan**: Pro includes 30 connected accounts and bills extra accounts by band
  beyond that; Creator stops at 5. See [Plans & billing](/concepts/billing).
- **Use idempotency keys** on `POST /posts` and message sends, so a retry from
  your queue never posts twice.
