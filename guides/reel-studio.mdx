Reel Studio edits **reels**: you record yourself talking to the camera, upload the video to
your PlugKit media library, and ask your assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, through the
[PlugKit MCP server](/mcp/overview)) to make a reel of it. Captions, cuts, motion design and
the final vertical video are produced on PlugKit's servers.

## Two formats: Standard and Premium

| | Standard | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who edits | your assistant, with you, step by step | a team of specialised AI editors on our servers — one plans, builders make each scene, a director designs the opening, a reviewer checks it all |
| What you get | clean cut, captions, motion design, end screen | cinematic motion design synced to your words, a designed opening, captions, a quality check — our best quality |
| How long | a few minutes per step, you approve each one | about an hour (sometimes two) in one go, nothing to approve meanwhile |
| Price | from 0.5 credit | from 4 credits, quoted before it starts |

The difference is the quality of the result: in Standard your own assistant does the edit;
in Premium a team of editors, each with its own job, does it for you. You can pick your
default in **Billing › Video credits**; your assistant uses it unless you ask for the other one.

## Credits

Reel Studio is paid in **video credits**, separately from your plan.

| Pack | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 10 credits | $15 |
| 30 credits | $39 |
| 100 credits | $99 |

- **Included:** new accounts get **2 credits**, and every month your plan includes **3**
  (Creator) or **10** (Pro).
- **Where to see them:** your balance and history are in **Billing › Video credits**.
- **Buying:** your assistant can give you a checkout link when you run low.

Credits are reserved when a step starts and charged when it succeeds. **If a step fails, its
credits come back automatically.**

## What things cost

### Standard

| What you ask | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Final reel with captions | 0.5 |
| + motion design (visuals, animated text, end screen) | +0.5 |
| Extra openings to A/B test (per run) | 0.5 |
| Sound effects | 0.25 (0.05 per re-mix) |

The derush, cuts and drafts are included (up to 5 drafts and 10 cuts per project). Your
assistant tells you the total of what you asked for before it starts.

### Premium

You pay for what you ask, for a recording up to 45 seconds:

| What you ask | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Captions only (cut + captions by our editors) | 4 |
| A few strong visuals on the key moments | 9 |
| The full Premium edit | 15 |
| Two extra openings to A/B test | +3 |
| A version with sound effects | +1 |

- **Longer recordings:** the edit costs more because there are more scenes to build: ×1.5
  from 45 to 90 seconds, ×2 from 90 seconds to 3 minutes.
- **Rounding:** prices are rounded up to the half credit.

### Changes after delivery

Watched your Premium edit and want changes? Tell your assistant everything you'd like changed,
and it sends them as one round:

| Round of changes | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Small fixes — a word, a typo, a timing, a color, a line cut | 2 |
| Rebuild or add a few scenes (up to three) | 4 |
| A new overall look, or most scenes rebuilt | 8 (more on a long recording) |

## Quotes: you always know the price first

A Premium edit — and every round of changes — is **quoted before anything starts**:

1. **Your assistant collects what you want.** That's the level, the openings, the sound
   effects, or the changes.
2. **It asks PlugKit for the price.** PlugKit reads the length of your recording and returns
   the quote, line by line. Nothing is created or charged at this point.
3. **It tells you the total** and asks if that's OK.
4. **Only after your yes, the edit starts**, and exactly that amount is charged — never more.

PlugKit refuses to start a Premium edit without your confirmation of that exact amount:
your assistant can't approve it for you. If you change your mind about what you want, it
simply asks for a new quote.

<Info>
  If PlugKit can't read the length of your recording before the quote (some formats), the
  price covers up to 45 seconds. A longer recording is stopped before any work is done, the
  credits come back, and your assistant shows you the new price.
</Info>

Every Premium edit runs within a fixed budget of work. In the rare case the editors reach
it before their final checks, you still get the edit as built, your assistant tells you, and
you can ask for a fix.

## Limits

Reel Studio makes **reels only**: vertical videos of **3 minutes at most**.

| | Standard | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording you upload | up to 5 minutes (retakes and silences are cut) | up to 3 minutes |
| Final reel | up to 3 minutes | up to 3 minutes |

Long videos (YouTube videos, podcasts, a long recording edited as a whole) aren't supported
yet. To make a reel from a longer recording, trim the passage you want first — or make one
reel per clip. A recording that is too long is refused before anything is charged.
